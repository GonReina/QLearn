If 
$$
\tau = \frac{2\pi}{\eta\Omega\sqrt{n_0}}
$$
is the effective JC time. For $-\sigma_{y}\hat{X} - wait -\sigma_{y}\hat{X}$ the effective time is
$$
t_{eff} = \tau = \nu\eta \Omega_{T} dt_{syx} dt_{free}
$$
then
$$
 \nu\eta \Omega_{T} dt_{syx} dt_{free} = \frac{2\pi}{\eta\Omega\sqrt{n_0}}
$$
and therefore
$$
dt_{syx} = \frac{2\pi}{\Omega_{T}\Omega\sqrt{n_0}dt_{free}}
$$
So for $n_{trot}$ steps, 
$$
dt_{sxx} = \frac{\tau}{2*n_{trot}}
$$
Choose a $\tau_{free}$ to get $dt_{syx}$ and $dt_{sxx}$
